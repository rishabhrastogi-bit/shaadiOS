Delete your account and data

You can delete your ShaadiOS™ account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with your verification code.

By email

If you can’t access the app, email support@shaadios.app from the address or phone number linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We may ask you to verify your identity.

What happens next

  • Your account is deactivated immediately.
  • Your personal data, journal entries, documents and photos are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Shared data you created with a partner or co-parent is removed from your side; the other person keeps their own copy of what they entered.
  • We may retain limited records where the law requires it, such as payment and tax records.
