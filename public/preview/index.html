ShaadiOS

Care for it early. Decide clearly if you must.

Relationship, family and mental-health tools for Indian couples and parents, with mediators and counsellors available long before anyone needs a courtroom.

Remember

Keep the good visible.

A shared calendar for the dates that matter, ideas for small surprises, and a quiet log of the effort each of you puts in, so it doesn’t go unnoticed.

Understand

See it from their side.

Social feeds tend to show you more of what you already feel. ShaadiOS uses AI to do the opposite: it gently shares content that helps each of you understand what the other is going through, and closes the gaps that misunderstanding leaves.

Get help early

Talk to someone before it hardens.

Book a mediator or relationship counsellor while things can still be repaired, with outcomes you can act on and the wider family kept intact.

Parent together

One calendar for the children.

School runs, classes, vaccinations and reminders, shared between both parents. If you live apart, schedules follow your own agreement or a court’s order, with every handover kept on record.

Clarity

Facts, when decisions are hard.

A private, dated record of what happened, in your own words, from the very start. When a hard decision has to be made, you won’t be relying on memory alone.

Find help, in one place

Like booking a doctor. For your relationship.

There’s no single place today to find the right relationship professional. ShaadiOS brings them together: compare, explore your options, and book a consultation.
