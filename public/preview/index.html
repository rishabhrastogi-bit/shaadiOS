What ShaadiOS really does

Three quiet tools that change the story.

📒

The relationship log book

Log good days, hard days and what actually happened, in one tap. Over time it becomes a private, dated timeline of your relationship, and our AI turns it into plain-language summaries you can read together.

🪜

The help ladder

Get the right help long before lawyers: a conversation, then a mediator or counsellor, mental-health support, and advocates or emergency services only if needed.

  1. Talk together
  2. Mediator
  3. Counsellor
  4. Mental health
  5. Advocate
  6. Emergency
🧒

Co-parenting, organised

School pickups, custody schedules, vaccinations and expenses on one shared calendar. Export everything as a clear, factual report whenever you need it.

AI features give general guidance and summaries, not legal, medical or therapeutic advice.
