Questions

When does ShaadiOS launch?

We’re launching on iOS and Android in India first. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when it’s live.

I live outside India. Is ShaadiOS for me?

Yes. ShaadiOS is built for Indian couples and parents wherever they live, including long-distance and across time zones. Join the waitlist and tell us where you live: it decides which countries we launch in next.

Is it free?

The essentials are free. Optional concierge services such as counselling or mediation sessions are priced clearly before you book, and you only pay for what you choose.

Can my partner see everything I add?

No. You choose what is shared. Private journal entries and personal check-ins stay private unless you decide otherwise.

Do we both need the app?

No. ShaadiOS works for one person on their own. Inviting a partner or co-parent is optional.

Is ShaadiOS a law firm or therapy service?

No. ShaadiOS is a software tool. Concierge services connect you with independent professionals; ShaadiOS itself does not give legal advice or clinical treatment.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on our account deletion page. We delete your data within 30 days.
