ShaadiOS

For every chapter, and every day.

Relationship, family and wellbeing tools for Indian couples and parents: plan, parent and grow together, with counsellors and mediators whenever you want them.

Remember

Keep the good visible.

A shared calendar for the dates that matter, ideas for small surprises, and a quiet log of the effort each of you puts in, so it doesn’t go unnoticed.

Understand

See it from their side.

Social feeds tend to show you more of what you already feel. ShaadiOS uses AI to do the opposite: it gently shares content that helps each of you understand what the other is going through, and closes the gaps that misunderstanding leaves.

Expert support

Talk to someone, any time.

Book a counsellor, coach or mediator for a fresh perspective, whether things are going well or feel stuck. Early conversations keep the whole family close.

Parenting

Run the kids’ day, together.

School runs, classes, vaccinations and reminders on one shared board, with who’s doing what. Living together, it keeps the household running. Living apart, switch to a custody schedule that follows your agreement or a court’s order.

Your story

Moments, milestones, efforts.

A private, dated timeline in your own words: the celebrations, the efforts, the hard days too. Whenever you need to look back, it’s all there.

Find help, in one place

Experts for every stage of family life.

Couples coaching, counselling, parenting advice or mediation: find the right professional in one place, compare options and book a consultation.

  • Couples coaches
  • Mediators
  • Relationship counsellors
  • Therapists & mental health
  • Family advocates
  • Child specialists

ShaadiOS connects you with independent professionals; it is not itself a law firm or a medical provider.
