Privacy policy

This policy explains how ShaadiOS™ (“we”) handles your personal data when you use the ShaadiOS app and website at shaadios.app. We act as the Data Fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, phone number, email, date of birth, and your chosen situation (for example married, engaged, separated, parent).
  • Content you add: calendar events, check-in responses, moments and journal entries, special dates, documents, photos and notes.
  • Family data: if you add children, their names, dates of birth, school, vaccination and visitation details. You must be the child’s parent or lawful guardian to add this.
  • Payment data: processed by Razorpay. We receive payment status and a reference, not your card or UPI credentials.
  • Device and usage data: device type, app version, crash logs and basic analytics.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the app and its features and to personalise content to your situation.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have turned on.
  • To verify your identity and keep accounts secure.
  • To book and deliver concierge services you request.
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and comply with law.

We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

3. Consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you sign up and when you turn on specific features. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings; some features may then stop working.

4. Sharing

  • With your partner or co-parent only what you choose to share. Private entries stay private.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: cloud hosting, waitlist sign-up storage (Google Sheets), SMS and email delivery, push notifications (Apple, Google), payments (Razorpay), and crash reporting.
  • Concierge professionals (counsellors, mediators, lawyers) only the information you choose to send them when you book.
  • Authorities where required by law or a valid court order.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on secure cloud servers and encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to authorised staff. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example tax and payment records).

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, update, export and delete your data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to, and make a grievance. Write to support@shaadios.app.

8. Children

ShaadiOS is for adults aged 18 and over. Children’s information is only added by a parent or guardian for family logistics, and is never used for advertising or tracking.

9. Grievance officer

Grievance Officer, ShaadiOS. Email: support@shaadios.app. We respond within 7 days and resolve within 30 days.

10. Changes

If we make material changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
