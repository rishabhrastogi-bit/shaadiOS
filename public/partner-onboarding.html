7. Terms
  1. Payment. ShaadiOS collects your session fee from the client, plus a ShaadiOS booking fee of that the client pays (waived during early access).
  2. Commission. ShaadiOS keeps % of each session fee (before GST).
  3. Payouts. Monthly, by the 10th, for sessions held the month before, into the account above: your fees minus the commission and any refunds, and any tax deducted as the law requires.
  4. GST. If you charge GST, it’s added to your fee and shown to the client.
  5. Response times. You keep to the response times in section 5.
  6. Anonymity. Clients may book under a first name they choose. You won’t try to find out who they are, and you contact them only through ShaadiOS.
  7. Confidentiality. You keep everything about ShaadiOS clients, and ShaadiOS’s business, confidential. You’ll also sign a non-disclosure agreement.
  8. Safety. If a client may be at risk of harm, you follow your professional duties and tell ShaadiOS straight away.
  9. No recording. Sessions are never recorded by anyone.
  10. Clients stay with ShaadiOS. You won’t ask ShaadiOS clients to book or pay you outside ShaadiOS.
  11. Data. You handle client information under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and your professional rules. Your session notes stay with you.
  12. Badge and ending. You carry the “ShaadiOS Partner” badge while the agreement is in force. Either of us can end it with 30 days’ notice.
