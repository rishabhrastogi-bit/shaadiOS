Marriage counselling in India: near you, online, free, and how to choose

Marriage counselling isn’t only for couples on the edge. It’s a calm, private place to understand each other, and it works best when you go early. Here is what it is, how to find a counsellor near you or online, the free options, and how to choose.

What is marriage counselling?

Marriage counselling (also called couples or relationship counselling, and spelt “counseling” in American English) is when a trained counsellor helps two people understand each other, break unhelpful patterns and handle disagreements better. The counsellor doesn’t take sides or decide who is right. Their job is to help you both be heard.

You can go as a couple or on your own. Individual counselling about your relationship is common, especially when one partner isn’t ready to join.

Signs counselling could help

  • The same argument keeps coming back, about money, in-laws, chores or time, and never really ends.
  • You feel more like flatmates than partners, talking only about logistics.
  • Trust has been hurt, by a secret, a lie or an affair, and you want to see if it can be rebuilt.
  • A big change is coming: a baby, moving abroad, a parent moving in, a new job in another city.
  • Stress or low mood is spilling into the relationship (see mental health below).
  • After a separation, to learn to co-parent calmly.

Going early is a sign of care, not failure. Small problems are much easier to work on than ones that have hardened over years.

Marriage counselling near me, or online?

Near you. Most large cities, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune and Kolkata, have many private couples counsellors, clinics and hospital psychology departments. To find one nearby, search for a counsellor or clinical psychologist in your area, check their qualifications (below), and ask whether they work with couples.

Online. Online marriage counselling by video works well for many couples. It saves travel, fits around work, and lets you choose from counsellors anywhere in India, in your language. It’s also the easiest option when you live in different cities.

Living abroad? Many NRI couples, in Singapore, the Gulf, the UK, the US and elsewhere, see an Indian counsellor online, someone who understands both cultures and family expectations back home. Check the session time across time zones when you book.

Free and government marriage counselling

  • Family courts: under the Family Courts Act, 1984, family courts can work with counsellors, and courts often refer couples to counselling or mediation before a case goes further.
  • Legal Services Authorities: District Legal Services Authorities offer free family counselling and mediation. Call 15100 (NALSA) for free legal aid.
  • Tele-MANAS: 14416. The Government of India’s free mental-health helpline, 24×7, in many languages.
  • iCall: 9152987821. Free counselling by phone and email from TISS (check their site for current hours).

If you feel unsafe with your partner, joint counselling is not the right first step. Your safety comes first: in an emergency call 112.

Pre-marriage (premarital) counselling

Premarital counselling helps couples talk through the big things before the wedding, while it’s still easy to be curious rather than defensive:

  • Expectations about living with or near parents and in-laws.
  • Money: earning, saving, spending, and supporting family.
  • Careers, moving cities or countries, and household chores.
  • Children: whether, when, and how you’d raise them.
  • How each of you handles conflict, and what you need when upset.

A few sessions are usually enough. It suits arranged and love marriages alike.

Family counselling

Sometimes the strain isn’t only between two people. Family counselling brings in parents, in-laws or children, to sort out roles, boundaries and communication in the whole family. It can help when a parent moves in, around caring for elders, or when children are struggling with changes at home.

When it’s also about mental health

Relationship strain and mental health often affect each other. If you or your partner feel persistently low, anxious, can’t sleep, or lose interest in things, mental health counselling for that person can help as much as couples work, and often alongside it. A clinical psychologist or psychiatrist can assess depression or anxiety properly.

For free support at any hour, call Tele-MANAS on 14416.

What happens in the first session

A first session usually lasts 45–60 minutes, online or in person. Most counsellors will:

  1. Explain how they work, how confidentiality works and its limits (for example, if someone may be at risk of harm).
  2. Ask each of you what brought you here and what you hope will change.
  3. Ask about your history together, families and stresses.
  4. Sometimes meet each of you alone for part of the time.
  5. Suggest a plan: how often to meet and something small to try before the next session.

Most couples meet for several sessions. It’s fine to tell the counsellor what is and isn’t working, or to try someone else if it doesn’t feel right.

How to choose a relationship counsellor in India

  • Qualifications. Look for a postgraduate degree in psychology or counselling. Clinical psychologists in India are registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), and you can ask for the registration number.
  • Couples training. Ask whether they’re trained in couples methods, such as the Gottman Method or Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT).
  • Language, culture and faith. Choose someone you can talk to comfortably, in your language, who understands joint families and in-laws. If faith matters to you, some counsellors work in a way that respects your religion; ask about this, and still check their qualifications.
  • Fees and policies. Fees vary widely. Ask about the fee, session length and cancellation rules before you book.
  • Fit. The most important thing is that you both feel safe and respected. One or two sessions usually tell you.

Questions people ask

What is marriage counselling?

Marriage counselling is when a trained counsellor helps a couple understand each other, break unhelpful patterns and handle disagreements better, without taking sides. You can go together or on your own.

How do I find marriage counselling near me?

Search for counsellors or clinical psychologists in your area who work with couples, check their qualifications (clinical psychologists are registered with the RCI), and ask about fees. If no one suitable is nearby, online marriage counselling lets you choose from counsellors across India.

Does online marriage counselling work?

Online counselling by video works well for many couples. It saves travel, fits around work, and is often the easiest option for couples in different cities or living abroad.

Is there free or government marriage counselling in India?

Yes. Family courts often refer couples to counselling or mediation, District Legal Services Authorities offer free family counselling and mediation (call NALSA on 15100), Tele-MANAS (14416) is the government’s free 24×7 mental-health helpline, and iCall (9152987821) offers free counselling.

What is premarital counselling?

Premarital or pre-marriage counselling helps couples talk through expectations before the wedding: in-laws and living arrangements, money, careers, children, and how each of you handles conflict. A few sessions are usually enough.

Can I go to marriage counselling alone?

Yes. Individual counselling about your relationship is common and helpful, especially when your partner isn’t ready to join.

Is marriage counselling confidential?

Yes. Counsellors keep what you share private, with limited exceptions such as a risk of serious harm. Ask your counsellor to explain their confidentiality rules in the first session.
