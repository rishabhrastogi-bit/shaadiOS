Relationship counselling in India: when to go, what happens and how to choose

Counselling isn’t only for couples on the edge. It is a calm, private place to understand each other better, and it works best when you go early. Here is what to expect and how to find the right person.

What is relationship counselling?

Relationship counselling (also called couples or marriage counselling) is when a trained professional helps two people understand each other, break unhelpful patterns and handle disagreements better. The counsellor doesn’t take sides or decide who is right. Their job is to help you both be heard.

You can go as a couple, or on your own. Individual counselling about your relationship is common and useful, especially when one partner isn’t ready to join.

Signs counselling could help

  • The same argument keeps coming back, about money, in-laws, chores or time, and never really ends.
  • You feel more like flatmates than partners, talking only about logistics.
  • Trust has been hurt, by a secret, a lie or an affair, and you want to see if it can be rebuilt.
  • A big change is coming: marriage, a baby, moving abroad, a parent moving in, a new job in another city.
  • Before marriage, to talk through expectations about family, money, careers and children (premarital counselling).
  • After a separation, to learn to co-parent calmly, even if you won’t be a couple again.

Going early is a sign of care, not failure. Small problems are much easier to work on than ones that have hardened over years.

What happens in the first session

A first session usually lasts 45–60 minutes, online or in person. Most counsellors will:

  1. Explain how they work, how confidentiality works and its limits (for example, if someone may be at risk of harm).
  2. Ask each of you what brought you here and what you hope will change.
  3. Ask about your history together, families and stresses.
  4. Sometimes meet each of you alone for part of the time.
  5. Suggest a plan: how often to meet and something small to try before the next session.

Most couples meet for several sessions. It’s fine to tell the counsellor what is and isn’t working for you, or to try someone else if it doesn’t feel right.

How to choose a counsellor in India

  • Qualifications. Look for a postgraduate degree in psychology or counselling. Clinical psychologists in India are registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), and you can ask for the registration number.
  • Couples training. Ask whether they are trained in couples methods, such as the Gottman Method or Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT).
  • Language and culture. Choose someone you can talk to comfortably, in your language, who understands joint families, in-laws and Indian expectations.
  • Online or in person. Online sessions work well for many couples and are easier with busy schedules, or when you live in different cities.
  • Fees and policies. Fees vary widely. Ask about the fee, session length and cancellation rules before you book.
  • Fit. The most important thing is that you both feel safe and respected. One or two sessions usually tell you.

Free and low-cost support

  • Tele-MANAS: 14416. The Government of India’s free mental-health helpline, 24×7, in many languages.
  • iCall: 9152987821. Free counselling by phone and email from TISS (check their site for current hours).
  • Family disputes: District Legal Services Authorities offer family counselling and mediation. Call 15100 for free legal aid.

If you feel unsafe with your partner, joint counselling is not the right first step. Your safety comes first: in an emergency call 112.

Questions people ask

Is couples counselling only for relationships in crisis?

No. Many couples go to understand each other better, before marriage, around a baby or a move, or to stop a repeating argument. Going early usually makes counselling easier and shorter.

Can I go to relationship counselling alone?

Yes. Individual counselling about your relationship is common and helpful, especially when your partner isn’t ready to join.

How many counselling sessions do couples need?

It varies. Many couples meet for several sessions; your counsellor will suggest a plan after the first one or two meetings.

Is relationship counselling confidential?

Yes. Counsellors keep what you share private, with limited exceptions such as a risk of serious harm. Ask your counsellor to explain their confidentiality rules in the first session.

How do I find a good marriage counsellor in India?

Look for a postgraduate qualification in psychology or counselling (clinical psychologists are registered with the RCI), training in couples methods such as Gottman or EFT, a language you’re comfortable in, and clear fees. Online sessions work well for many couples.

Is there free counselling in India?

Yes. Tele-MANAS (14416) is the Government of India’s free 24×7 mental-health helpline, and iCall (9152987821) offers free counselling. For family disputes, call 15100 for free legal aid, including family counselling and mediation.
