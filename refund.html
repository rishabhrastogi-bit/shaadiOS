Refund & cancellation policy

This policy applies to paid services purchased through ShaadiOS™, such as counselling, mediation or consultation bookings made via our Concierge.

Cancellations

  • Cancel or reschedule free of charge at least 24 hours before the scheduled session.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours of the session, or no-shows, may not be eligible for a refund.

Refunds

  • If we or the professional cancel a session, you get a full refund or a free reschedule, your choice.
  • If a session could not take place because of a technical fault on our side, you get a full refund.
  • Services already delivered are not refundable, but if you were unhappy, write to us within 7 days and we will review it.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

How to request one

Email support@shaadios.app with your registered phone number and the booking details. We reply within 2 business days.
