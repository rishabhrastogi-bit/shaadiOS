How it works

Pick your chapter

Married, engaged, separated or co-parenting. One honest question at sign-up shapes the whole app.

Invite if you want

Use it on your own, or invite a partner or co-parent to share just the parts you choose.

Small rituals, big difference

Daily one-tap moments, quarterly check-ins, reminders, and help when you need it.

Tools for the things that matter

📅

Life calendar & reminders

Birthdays, anniversaries, bills, school events and handoffs, with nudges that respect quiet hours.

💚

Quarterly check-ins

Six gentle questions, two minutes, one honest picture of how things are going, with no scorekeeping.

☀️

“How’s today going?”

One tap to log a good day, a deep talk or a hard moment. Over time it becomes your shared story.

👶

Co-parenting hub

Custody and visitation days, school, vaccinations and shared expenses, kept separate from each parent’s private space.

🗂️

Document vault

Marriage certificate, health records, insurance and school papers, encrypted and in one place.

📒

Event & moment log

A calm, dated record of what happened. Private journal entries stay private.

🤝

Concierge support

Reach counselling, mediation or a family-law consult before things escalate. Priced clearly before you book.

🔒

Privacy controls

Face ID lock, hide your journal from your partner, and export or delete everything at any time.

🔄

Life changes, your data stays

Switch chapters any time and the app adapts without starting over.
